🏔️ TourEase – Explore Uttarakhand with Local Experiences

Discover hidden destinations. Connect with local communities. Travel smarter.

TourEase is a tourism platform designed to make exploring the beautiful Garhwal and Kumaon regions of Uttarakhand easier, more affordable, and more accessible. It aims to connect tourists with local homestays, guides, transport providers, and unique local experiences.

The platform focuses on promoting lesser-known destinations, supporting local businesses, and helping travellers plan trips according to their budget, interests, and available time.

📌 Problem Statement

Many beautiful destinations in Uttarakhand remain unexplored because tourists often lack reliable information about local stays, transportation, guides, and experiences.

Some major challenges include:

Limited visibility of local tourism providers.

Difficulty finding affordable accommodation and transportation.

Lack of personalized travel planning.

Poor connectivity in remote tourist destinations.

Overcrowding at popular tourist locations.

Limited promotion of hidden and offbeat destinations.

💡 Our Solution

TourEase aims to provide a single platform where tourists can discover destinations, plan trips, and connect with local service providers.

The proposed platform includes:

Personalized AI-based trip planning.

Budget-friendly travel recommendations.

Discovery of local homestays and hotels.

Local guide and transportation connections.

Promotion of hidden and offbeat destinations.

Support for local communities and small businesses.

✨ Features

1. Personalized Trip Planner

Users can plan their trips based on:

Destination or region.

Number of travel days.

Daily budget.

Personal interests.

2. Explore Uttarakhand

Discover different tourism regions, including:

Garhwal High Hills.

Kumaon Lake Belt.

Char Dham Corridor.

Valley of Flowers and Bhyundar.

Offbeat Village Circuits.

3. Interest-Based Recommendations

Users can select their preferred activities:

Trekking and Nature.

Temples and Pilgrimage.

Food and Markets.

Offbeat Villages.

Adventure Sports.

Wellness and Slow Travel.

4. Local Tourism Support

TourEase aims to connect travellers with:

Local homestays.

Tour guides.

Transportation providers.

Local businesses.

Cultural and adventure experiences.

5. Budget-Friendly Planning

The planner allows users to select a daily budget between ₹800 and ₹6,000 and generates a sample itinerary based on their preferences.

⚙️ How It Works

The proposed TourEase workflow:

User enters travel preferences.

The system analyses budget, destination, duration, and interests.

Suitable destinations and activities are recommended.

A personalized itinerary is generated.

Users can explore and connect with local service providers.

Users share feedback and reviews.
