Feasibility and viability

Built to survive patchy signal and slow trust.

An honest look at what makes this workable now, where it can break, and how we're handling it.

Why it's feasible

  • Built on existing web, AI, GPS and cloud technology
  • Prototype can start with a handful of selected destinations
  • Providers onboard gradually, not all at once
  • Same architecture scales region by region
  • Low-data design supports remote areas

Where it can break

  • Incorrect or outdated tourism information
  • Low digital literacy among some providers
  • Weak network coverage in remote locations
  • Slow adoption in the early stages
  • Building trust and consistent service quality

How we handle it

  • Provider verification plus admin moderation
  • Simple UI with assisted onboarding for providers
  • Cached essential information and lightweight pages
  • Pilot phase with local tourism partners
  • Ratings, reviews and a reporting layer
